About SolarShare

A clear digital view of shared community solar energy.

☀
SolarShare connects solar generation, household allocation and energy usage.

The idea

SolarShare is designed for a community rooftop solar installation where one common generation source is shared among multiple households.

The system records generation, household consumption and allocation ratios, then presents a monthly net usage summary.
